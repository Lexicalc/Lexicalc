Lexicalc

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Lexicalc is a free letters and numbers game, in English, available at lexicalc.github.io. This page explains what data the game uses, why, and how to exercise your rights.

In short: no ads, no analytics, no data sold. Without an account, everything stays on your device. With an account, we keep only what the game needs to work, and you can delete everything whenever you want.

1. Controller and contact

The data controller is the publisher of Lexicalc. For any question about your data, write to lexicalc.jeu@gmail.com.

We read every message, but we can't reply to each one individually. Requests about your personal data (access, correction, deletion…) will always be answered within one month.

2. Playing without an account (guest)

You can play without creating an account. Your results, language, player name and settings then stay only in your browser's local storage, on your device. They are not sent to us.

There are two exceptions:

  • in live multiplayer, the name you choose and your answers are sent to the other players in the room during the game. They pass through our provider Supabase but are not stored;
  • when you start a Daily Game round, the fair-play information described in section 5 is sent (date, round, random device identifier, scrambled connection code, result and time).

3. Playing with an account

When you create an account, we store:

  • your email address and your password. The password is irreversibly encrypted: nobody, not even us, can read it;
  • your nickname and your friend code;
  • your friends list and friend requests;
  • your results: Daily Game, records, games played, duels, turn-based matches and multiplayer games;
  • technical account information: creation and last sign-in dates.

This data is used to create and secure your account, keep your results across devices, display the leaderboards, and let you add friends and challenge them. Your nickname and results are visible to your friends in the friends' leaderboards, and to the players you play against (duels, matches, multiplayer rooms). Other players only see your Daily Game result anonymously, mixed with everyone else's, in a percentage such as “you did better than 72% of players”. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Legal basis: performance of the service you request by creating an account, and our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure and working properly.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications in ⚙️ Settings, we store the notification address provided by your browser for this phone (with its encryption keys), the game language and the phone's time zone (so that nothing is sent at night). Notifications go through your browser's push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft) and are end-to-end encrypted: that service cannot read them. You can turn them off at any time in Settings; they are also switched off when you log out.

4. Emails

Your address is used to confirm your account, reset your password and confirm a change of address. We may also occasionally write to you to announce an important new feature of the game. To stop receiving these announcements, simply reply "stop" to one of them.

Your address is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers.

5. Game activity monitoring and fair play

The publisher has an administration screen showing usage statistics: number of players, games played and account activity. They are used only to follow how the game is doing and to improve it.

Fair play. To keep the Daily Game leaderboards fair, the game records, for each Daily Game round started: the date, the round, a random identifier created by the game for your device (it contains no personal information and is not linked to your phone's hardware), a scrambled code computed from your internet connection (your IP address itself is never stored), your result and your time. With an account, the game also notes which of these device identifiers your account has been used on.

This lets the game allow only one try per round and per account, show a result as unranked when the same device has already been used by another account for that round, and flag unusual results (for example an impossible time, or two accounts that seem to be used by the same person) to the publisher, who reviews them. Nothing is blocked or deleted automatically. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the competition fair.

6. No ads, no trackers

Lexicalc shows no ads and uses no advertising cookies or analytics tools. Your device's local storage is used only to make the game work: sign-in, language, settings and results waiting to be sent.

7. Service providers

To work, Lexicalc relies on these services:

  • Supabase: database, accounts and multiplayer;
  • Google (Gmail): sending the game's emails;
  • Browser push services (Google, Apple, Mozilla, Microsoft): delivering encrypted notifications, if you turned them on;
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting. Like any host, GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses in its technical logs;
  • jsDelivr: delivery of technical libraries and word lists.

Some of these providers are located in the United States. These transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards required by data protection law, such as standard contractual clauses or recognised data protection frameworks.

8. Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your address, nickname, friends and results are erased immediately and permanently. Any technical backups of the database are automatically overwritten after a few days.

Fair-play information (section 5) is kept for 60 days, and device identifiers linked to an account for 6 months. It is deleted with your account.

9. Your rights

Under data protection law, you can access your data, correct it, have it erased, request a copy (portability), object to its processing or ask for it to be restricted.

  • Your nickname, email address and password can be changed directly in the game: Menu → Profile;
  • To delete your account: account deletion page, or Menu → Profile → Delete account;
  • For any other request about your data: lexicalc.jeu@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Minimum age

Anyone can play Lexicalc without an account. To create an account, you must be at least 15, or the age set by the law of your country. Below that age, a parent's consent is required.

11. Security

All connections to the game are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are never stored in plain text, and access to data is protected by rules that only let each player access what concerns them.

12. Changes

This policy may change as the game evolves. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page. In case of a significant change, players with an account will be informed.
